I am Indrajit Kurmi, a Computer Vision Researcher with a strong foundation in computational photography, synthetic aperture imaging, and drone-based sensing. My passion lies in developing vision-based solutions for complex real-world challenges through a combination of research, prototyping, and deployment. 

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Johannes Kepler University, Linz, where my research focused on model-based occlusion removal for synthetic aperture imaging. Over the years, I have contributed to projects that merge AI, computer vision, and autonomous systems, particularly in areas such as airborne optical sectioning, search and rescue, and anomaly detection. 

My goal is to continue advancing the frontiers of computer vision and machine learning toward practical, scalable applications that positively impact society.
